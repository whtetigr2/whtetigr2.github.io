Multiplies every off-diagonal; the diagonal stays put. Below the dominance wall you are sampling. Just past it the chain quenches into two-level domains; push further and the world goes flat. The usable window is only ~0.005 wide — watch the margin readout and step with arrow keys.
Coupling period80
A band straddling the wall is a repeating quench — the only thing here that makes large structure. Two measured rules: low end well under the wall (0.95 works, 1.05 goes flat) and period near 80. Gets lag-1 0.99 against 0.26 held still.
