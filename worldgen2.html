Z1's real connection rules (1,0) (2,1) (2,3) (4,1), each with four rotations. Every offset has dx+dy odd, so the lattice is a chessboard — the chip is bipartite by construction, which is why a sweep is two parallel steps.

What you are looking at

Every tile is sampled from an Ising energy conditioned on the tiles already placed. Perlin gets an unbounded world free because it is a function of coordinates; a sampler earns one by conditioning, which is the operation it is best at.
The browser sampler is checked against THRML on the same stencil: largest deviation 0.027 in nearest-neighbour correlation across β=0.18–0.40. It runs the model, not an impression of it.
Seam bond measures z = −1.4 against the interior distribution at a 2-cell blanket, and −3.0 at 1 cell — exactly as the stencil's reach predicts. Turn on borders and try to find a join.
